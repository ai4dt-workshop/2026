Artificial Intelligence for Digital Twins: Challenges and Best Practices

A half-day session bringing together researchers and practitioners to share advances in AI-driven digital twins — and to critically examine the challenges, lessons learned, and best practices from real-world deployments.

📅 October 21–23, 2026 📍 Maastricht University, The Netherlands ⏱️ 4.5 hour workshop session
View agenda Contact organizers
Abstract

About the session

A digital twin is a software system that tightly couples physical assets with real-time data, simulation, and AI-driven analytics, and is rapidly emerging as a foundational technology across industry, infrastructure, and science. This session aims to bring together researchers and practitioners to share recent advances in integrating AI into digital twin architectures, while critically examining the challenges, lessons learned, and best practices arising from real-world deployments.

🎯 Goals

Present and discuss original research on AI-enabled digital twins, identify open challenges and emerging best practices in the field, and foster community building among Benelux and international researchers.

👥 Intended audience

Researchers, industry practitioners, students, and policymakers working at the intersection of AI and cyber-physical / digital twin systems.

📖 Publication venue

Authors of selected session papers will be invited to submit extended versions to Digital Twins and Applications (IET/Wiley), subject to the journal's standard peer review process. Official support has been obtained from the journal.

Call for contributions

Topics of interest

Contributors are expected to address topics including, but not limited to:

Modeling & simulation

AI-driven modeling and simulation within digital twin architectures.

Monitoring & maintenance

Machine learning methods for real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance.

Explainable & trustworthy AI

Explainability and trustworthiness for digital twin applications.

Multi-agent & federated DTs

Multi-agent and federated approaches for distributed digital twins.

Domain applications

Manufacturing, smart cities, healthcare, energy, and other application domains.

Evaluation & benchmarks

Evaluation frameworks, benchmarks, and emerging best practices.
